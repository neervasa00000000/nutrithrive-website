# NutriThrive search-growth baseline — 15 September 2026

## Scope and safety

This is a repository baseline, not a guarantee of rankings or a substitute for Google Search Console, Bing Webmaster Tools, Merchant Center or GA4 account data. The exact pre-work baseline was five modified blog files plus two untracked audit directories. Those files were preserved.

## Technical

- Sitemap: 151 canonical locations; local sitemap validator passed before implementation.
- Indexability: priority URLs inspected locally are indexable, self-canonical and in the sitemap. Search-engine index state still requires account inspection.
- Canonicals: priority manifest shows self-referencing public canonicals; no canonical or URL changes were authorised.
- Robots before: broad public allow; explicit Googlebot, Bingbot and DuckDuckBot access; ChatGPT and Perplexity search were effectively allowed by the general rule; obsolete Anthropic names were present.
- Product schema before: six PDPs expose Product and Offer data. Moringa has three Offer variants. Its Product also carried a 4.9/12 AggregateRating sourced from business-wide Google reviews; this was not product-safe.
- Article schema before: 122 Article records; 42 missing `datePublished`, 36 missing `dateModified`, zero missing author in the local scan.
- Redirects: existing Netlify redirect architecture present and unchanged in this run.
- Prices: moringa 100g $11; moringa 200g $21.50; moringa 400g $35; curry leaves 30g $7; Darjeeling black tea 100g $7.50; moringa soap 95g $7; combo $17; gift pack $35.
- Shipping: canonical free Australian shipping threshold is $79+ and was not changed.

## Search observations

These are one-session observations preserved from `docs/seo/multi-discovery/SEARCH-ENGINE-TESTS.csv`, not stable rank facts.

### Curry

- Bing: `dried curry leaves Australia` showed the guide near #1 and PDP near #3.
- Bing: `buy curry leaves Australia` did not show NutriThrive in the observed top ten.
- Bing: `curry leaf substitute` near #3; `fresh vs dried curry leaves` near #2; `curry leaves vs curry powder` near #2; `how to store curry leaves` near #8.
- DuckDuckGo was less consistent; the Curry PDP appeared for buying/dried queries but snippets could be stale.

### Darjeeling

- Bing: broad Australia guide near #3 for informational and buying queries; the PDP was weak/absent for the buying query.
- Bing: brewing page not observed in the top ten; broad guide surfaced instead of the dedicated English Breakfast comparison; caffeine guide near #4.
- DuckDuckGo visibility was inconsistent and sometimes stale.

### Moringa

- Bing: powder PDP near #4 for `moringa powder Australia`; homepage and PDP held branded visibility.
- Bing: shelf-life guide near #2 and choose guide near #1 in the observed session.
- Moringa content remains under a regulatory/compliance firewall: no health, dosage, disease, pregnancy, child or therapeutic expansion in this phase.
