# 90-day growth roadmap

## Days 0–14 — foundation

Confirm Bing/Search Console/Merchant ownership; inspect priority URLs; validate Bing and Google sitemaps; decide IndexNow only after the gate; verify current AI crawler access in hosting logs; resolve remaining evidence-backed Article dates; validate Product/Offer truth; confirm returns, shipping and business hours; run a non-charged GA4 funnel test.

## Days 15–30 — commercial discovery

Use account evidence to diagnose Curry and Darjeeling PDP discovery before copy changes. Resolve Merchant/free-listing errors, image requirements, product identifiers and feed/landing-page mismatches. Create small snippet-test candidates only for high-impression, weak-CTR URLs. Improve user-journey links only where a clear next step exists.

## Days 31–60 — authority and information gain

Collect real farm, harvest, drying, packing, product and brewing evidence. Run founder-controlled curry cooking/storage tests and document methods. Verify Darjeeling provenance. Add primary sources to objective claims without citation dumping. Research legitimate Australian editorial/community opportunities; no paid links or spam outreach.

## Days 61–90 — scale winners

Scale only experiments that improve qualified Australian sessions, product-view rate, add-to-cart, checkout and contribution profit. Expand winning Merchant images, query families, AI citation patterns and authority assets. Stop or revise losing experiments; do not mass-produce blogs or pages.

## Guardrails throughout

One intent has one primary URL. Update before consolidating; consolidate before creating. No fake reviews, dates, certifications, evidence, organic claims, scarcity, health expansion, doorway pages, keyword stuffing, mass title changes, payment changes or ranking guarantees.
