# NutriThrive Blog Change Lock Policy

## Default rule

After a meaningful SEO or content change, lock the page for 28 days. During the lock, do not casually change its URL, title, H1, primary intent or major structure.

## Statuses

- UNTOUCHED: no proposal recorded.
- PROPOSED: evidence-backed change awaiting approval.
- APPROVED: founder approved, not yet implemented.
- IMPLEMENTED: shipped and awaiting baseline capture.
- MEASURING: inside the 28-day observation period.
- LOCKED: must not change before review unless an exception applies.
- READY_FOR_REVIEW: measurement period complete.

## Exceptions

Legal or regulatory risk, factual errors, incorrect prices or shipping, broken links/pages, serious technical defects and severe UX problems may bypass the lock. Record the reason and exact scope.

## Required implementation record

Record the implementation date, URL, exact changes, reason, baseline GSC source and metrics, GA4 baseline if available, expected outcome and review date.

## Content governance

`UPDATE > CONSOLIDATE > CREATE`. Do not create a new URL until the master map proves that no existing page owns the intent.
