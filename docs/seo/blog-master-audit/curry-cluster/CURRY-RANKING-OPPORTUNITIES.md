# Curry-leaf ranking opportunities

Decision date: 15 September 2026  
Scope: future recommendations only; nothing in this document has been implemented.

## Executive verdict

NutriThrive does not need more curry-leaf URLs. It already has 15 curry articles plus a PDP and category page. The realistic opportunity is to make the existing pages more distinct, more useful and more demonstrably experienced.

The strongest current evidence is:

- substitute guide: 0 clicks / 93 impressions / average position 10.67 in the latest filtered GSC view;
- fresh-vs-dried: 0/5 at 4.8 recently and 0/9 at 3.44 in a longer filtered view;
- curry leaves vs curry powder: 0/9 at 9.78 recently and 0/39 at 15.69 longer-term;
- storage: 0/2 at 3.5;
- recipes: 0/4 at 9.25;
- the live research set surfaced the NutriThrive PDP for transactional dried-leaf searches and several NutriThrive specialist guides for informational searches.

These exports are filtered and overlapping. They indicate opportunities but must not be added together or treated as total traffic.

## Top five realistic opportunities

### 1. Protect and improve the substitute page

- **Why:** It has the clearest measured demand in this cluster: 93 filtered impressions with no clicks and an average position near page one.
- **Future smallest change:** After the lock, review the search snippet and opening answer against the exact query. Preserve the honest “no exact substitute” position; make the choice logic scannable by dish and constraint. Do not add another substitute URL.
- **Information gain:** A founder-tested dish table could compare “closest aroma,” “easy Australian supermarket option,” and “better to omit.” **FOUNDER ASSET REQUIRED** for any claimed cooking result.
- **Commercial path:** Offer the dried product as the authentic option only after answering the emergency substitution problem.
- **Confidence:** HIGH on opportunity; MEDIUM on the exact future edit until query-by-page data is supplied.

### 2. Remove the broad guide’s competition with the PDP and specialist guides

- **Why:** `/blog/dried-curry-leaves-australia-guide` explicitly targets “Buy, Store & Use,” which overlaps the PDP, storage page, fresh-vs-dried page and ways-to-use page. Its H2s reproduce all of those roles.
- **Future smallest change:** Keep the URL and turn it into a short Australian primer/hub. Give one-paragraph orientation and route the full answers to specialist pages. Stop treating “buy dried curry leaves Australia” as its primary job.
- **Preserve:** URL, canonical, indexability, useful inbound equity and accurate origin/format context.
- **Confidence:** HIGH for the overlap diagnosis. No redirect recommendation without page-by-query evidence.

### 3. Defend fresh-vs-dried with original cooking evidence

- **Why:** The page already appeared around positions 3–5 in small filtered samples but earned no click. It is the cluster’s nearest visible win.
- **Future smallest change:** Keep the search job and add only evidence that competitors cannot copy: the same dish made with fresh and dried leaves, exact starting quantities, photos and honest sensory notes.
- **Do not do:** claim equivalence, superiority or a universal conversion ratio from unverified opinion.
- **FOUNDER ASSET REQUIRED:** side-by-side ingredient and finished-dish photos, dated test notes and the exact recipe used.
- **Confidence:** HIGH on priority; evidence-dependent on execution.

### 4. Separate complete recipes from quick usage ideas

- **Why:** Recipe SERPs favour complete task structures—time, servings, ingredients, steps, ratings and original food photography. NutriThrive has the right two assets but must keep their roles clear.
- **Future smallest change:** Complete recipes stay on `/curry-leaves-recipes-beyond-dal`; 30–60 second tempering and quick meal ideas stay on `/curry-leaves-in-australian-cooking-how-to-use-2026`. Cross-link them using intent language.
- **FOUNDER ASSET REQUIRED:** real tested recipes and original dish/process photos.
- **Confidence:** MEDIUM because page-by-query GSC data is unavailable.

### 5. Strengthen PDP differentiation without competing on cheapest price

- **Why:** Transactional results contain materially cheaper and larger packs. Indian Spices lists 50 g for $3.50; Tales of India lists 50 g for $1.99; Hindustan Imports offers bulk sizes. Spice Zen competes with an organic badge. NutriThrive’s 30 g at $7 cannot credibly own “cheapest.”
- **Future smallest change:** Keep the PDP’s purchase focus and make real evidence easy to inspect: exact pack, own-farm provenance, processing, Melbourne packing, actual product photos and transparent non-organic status. Route learning questions to guides.
- **FOUNDER ASSET REQUIRED:** real farm/harvest/drying/packing photos or records before making those processes more prominent.
- **Confidence:** HIGH.

## Recipes versus ways-to-use verdict

**Verdict: KEEP BOTH, WITH HARD BOUNDARIES. Do not merge now.**

| Page | Required role | Content it may own | Content it should defer |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/blog/curry-leaves-recipes-beyond-dal` | Complete recipe collection | Ingredients, quantities, servings, timing and full methods for five named dishes | General “10 ways” brainstorm and broad ingredient education |
| `/blog/curry-leaves-in-australian-cooking-how-to-use-2026` | Technique + quick ideas | Tempering method and fast applications such as eggs, pasta, roast vegetables and garnish | Full recipe cards and long recipe methods |

Current HTML supports the distinction: the recipe page has five named recipes, while the ways page gives ten applications anchored by a common tempering technique. Query wording overlaps, so future headings and anchors must reinforce the different task. Confidence is MEDIUM until query-by-page GSC shows which URL Google selects for `curry leaf recipes`, `recipes using curry leaves` and `how to use curry leaves`.

## PDP cannibalisation assessment

### High risk

- `/blog/dried-curry-leaves-australia-guide`: title and sections include “buy,” “store” and “use,” while the PDP targets `Dried Curry Leaves Australia` and `Buy dried curry leaves Australia`.
- `/blog/nutrithrive-dried-curry-leaves-tradition-health`: combines farm story, health, fresh-vs-dried, recipes and “where to buy,” then links to the product repeatedly.

### Manageable support, not cannibalisation by itself

- Fresh-vs-dried, substitute, storage, curry-powder comparison, recipes and ways-to-use have different primary intent and can legitimately support the PDP.
- Repeated PDP links do not prove ranking cannibalisation, but 8–14 PDP links per article are already ample. Adding more generic product CTAs is unlikely to be the highest-value move.

### Required future boundary

- **PDP owns:** buy, price, pack size, stock, shipping, provenance, product evidence, add to cart.
- **Broad guide owns:** orientation and routing.
- **Specialists own:** one complete informational answer each.
- **Category owns:** discovery/navigation only.

## Future internal-link design (not implemented)

Use descriptive anchors that match the destination task, consistent with [Google’s link guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/links-crawlable).

1. Category → each specialist page by task label, not repeated “read more.”
2. Broad guide → PDP (`buy dried curry leaves`), fresh-vs-dried, storage, curry-vs-powder, substitute, recipes and ways-to-use.
3. Substitute ↔ curry-vs-powder for “not a substitute” clarification; substitute → PDP only after the answer.
4. Fresh-vs-dried → storage for preservation and → PDP for dried format.
5. Ways-to-use ↔ recipes: “complete curry-leaf recipes” one way and “quick ways to use curry leaves” the other.
6. Recipes → storage when readers have leftover leaves; recipe pages → PDP after ingredients/method context.
7. PDP → storage, fresh-vs-dried, recipes and ways-to-use, without reproducing their answers.
8. Health/heart/hair pages should link to culinary pages only where contextually useful; they must not become high-pressure product funnels.

## Smallest future change set

After the 13 October measurement lock and after obtaining query-by-page data:

1. Lock all primary jobs in the content registry.
2. Narrow only the broad guide and farm/tradition/health page; preserve URLs and canonicals.
3. Review snippet and opening answer for substitute, fresh-vs-dried and curry-vs-powder; avoid mass title changes.
4. Add task-specific original evidence to fresh-vs-dried, storage and recipes only after the founder supplies it.
5. Replace generic cross-links only where the anchor hides the destination task; do not increase link volume.
6. Re-measure query-by-page impressions, CTR, selected URL and assisted PDP clicks before considering consolidation.
