### Funnel implementation review

The current scripts contain and deduplicate the required events:

1. `view_item` on product pages (`_ntItemViewSent`)
2. `blog_product_click` on in-article product links; explicit tracked links are excluded from the delegated fallback to prevent duplicates
3. `add_to_cart` from the shared cart add method
4. `view_cart` on a non-empty cart (`_ntViewCartSent`)
5. `begin_checkout` on a non-empty checkout (`_ntBeginCheckoutSent`)
6. `add_shipping_info`, `add_payment_info`, and `purchase` are also implemented
7. Purchase events are deduplicated by PayPal order ID

The local browser journey passed from product to cart to checkout without submitting payment. Cart totals, the $79 free-shipping target, country shipping calculation, and PayPal/card mounts worked.
