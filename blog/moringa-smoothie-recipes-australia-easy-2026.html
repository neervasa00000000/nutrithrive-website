Moringa Smoothie Recipes Australia: Easy 2026 Guide

25 June 2026 · 6 min read · By Neer Vasa

Quick Answer

Start with ½ teaspoon (about 1–1.5 g) per serve if you are new. Experienced users often use 1 teaspoon. See our side effects guide before increasing dose.

Smoothies are the easiest way to use moringa powder without tasting lawn clippings. If you dose correctly and blend long enough. These three recipes use ingredients you can find at Woolworths or Coles.

Clump-free blending

Whisk powder with 1 tbsp warm water into a paste before adding cold fruit. Blend 45–60 seconds. Fat helps mouthfeel: yoghurt, nut butter, or avocado.

Recipe 1: Tropical green (beginner)

  • ½ tsp moringa powder
  • 1 frozen banana
  • ½ cup frozen mango
  • 1 cup milk (dairy or oat)
  • 1 tbsp Greek yoghurt

Recipe 2: Berry cacao (hides grassiness)

  • ½–1 tsp moringa powder
  • 1 cup frozen mixed berries
  • 1 tbsp cacao powder
  • 1 cup milk + 1 tsp honey or maple

Recipe 3: Green peanut satay (savoury-sweet)

  • ½ tsp moringa powder
  • 1 frozen banana
  • 1 tbsp peanut butter
  • 1 cup milk
  • Pinch cinnamon

More taste-masking blends

Three extras if you want sharper, greener, or creamier options beyond the Woolworths staples above. Stay at ½–1 tsp moringa.

Pineapple ginger lime (no banana)

  • 1 cup frozen pineapple
  • ½ cup cucumber
  • Juice of 1 lime + thumb of fresh ginger
  • 1 cup water + ½ tsp moringa

Acid and ginger shift the earthy note toward “green and zingy” rather than grassy.

Avocado mint (bright green)

  • ½ avocado + handful fresh mint
  • 1 frozen banana + 1 cup almond milk
  • ½ tsp moringa

Mint redirects the moringa taste; avocado keeps it creamy and photo-green.

Tropical protein (breakfast fill)

  • ½ cup frozen mango + ½ cup frozen pineapple
  • 1 cup coconut milk + 2 tbsp natural yoghurt
  • ½ tsp moringa

Yoghurt tang integrates with moringa; more filling than fruit-only blends.

FAQ

How much moringa powder in a smoothie?
Start with ½ teaspoon per serve; increase slowly if your stomach tolerates it.
How do I stop moringa clumping?
Paste with warm water first, then blend with fruit and liquid at high speed.
Best time for a moringa smoothie?
Morning with breakfast is easiest to repeat daily: consistency beats perfect timing.
What fruit hides the taste of moringa best?
Frozen mango and ripe banana mask best; pineapple works well too. Strong tropical sweetness does most of the work.
Can I put moringa in a smoothie every day?
Yes. Daily use at food amounts (½–1 tsp) is the usual approach for building habit and nutritional benefit over time.
