The Dermatologist Said My Acne Was Severe, and Discussed Prescription Options

I was 23. My face was covered in painful cystic acne.

Not cute little whiteheads. Deep, angry, under-the-skin cysts that hurt to touch. The kind that left scars. The kind that made strangers stare.

I'd tried everything: $200 dermatologist appointments (7 doctors), $85/month prescription creams, $60 Instagram "miracle" serums, birth control (made it worse), antibiotics (worked 2 months, then stopped).

Nothing worked long-term. The dermatologist discussed isotretinoin (a prescription medicine with serious risks and monitoring). I wanted to try lifestyle and gut work first, that was my choice, not medical advice for anyone else.

I spent months learning what might be driving my breakouts. For me, gut inflammation and diet triggers seemed to matter as much as topicals.

Over 12+ weeks of diet changes plus moringa as a food supplement, my skin looked noticeably better, not perfect for everyone. This is what I did (and what didn't).

Is This You? (Take the Test)

You likely have hormonal / gut-driven acne if:

  • Breakouts around jawline, chin, or neck
  • Deep, painful cysts (not surface whiteheads)
  • Acne flares 1 week before period
  • Started in your 20s (not teen years)
  • Topicals don't work (benzoyl peroxide, salicylic acid)
  • Stress makes it worse
  • Dairy = instant breakout
  • You also have bloating, irregular periods, or bad PMS

4+ checked: keep reading. 7+: this guide is for you.

Why Your Acne Won't Clear (What Dermatologists Don't Tell You)

Dermatologists say: bacteria + oil + clogged pores → use this cream. That's symptom management, like mopping while the sink overflows.

Hormonal acne = internal problem

  1. Gut is broken (leaky gut, bad bacteria)
  2. Systemic inflammation
  3. Inflammation triggers hormones (androgens, cortisol)
  4. More sebum + inflammation = cystic acne

For some people, improving gut health may help lower inflammation that affects skin, but this is not a guarantee, and severe acne still needs a dermatologist.

The Gut-Skin Connection (The Science)

Your gut controls: 70% of immune system, 90% of serotonin, hormone signalling, inflammation levels.

Broken gut → toxins leak (LPS), immune overreaction, cortisol + androgens rise, more oil, more acne.

What broke my gut

  • Antibiotics for acne (killed good bacteria)
  • Birth control (6 months)
  • Sugar, processed food, stress
  • Dairy every meal, gluten daily

$250 stool test results: zero beneficial bacteria, high pathogens, candida overgrowth, leaky gut markers (elevated zonulin), low digestive enzymes. My gut was a warzone.

What Helped My Skin (4 Things I Tried)

#1: Gut healing protocol (12 weeks)

Phase 1 (Weeks 1 to 4), kill bad bacteria: oregano oil, garlic extract, berberine. Zero sugar, gluten, dairy. Anti-inflammatory foods only.

Phase 2 (Weeks 5 to 8), rebuild: probiotic 50B CFU, prebiotic fiber, 2 tsp NutriThrive moringa (anti-inflammatory + prebiotic), sauerkraut, bone broth.

Phase 3 (Weeks 9 to 12+), maintain: probiotic 25B, daily moringa, omega-3, zinc. 80% whole foods.

My result: noticeably fewer new cysts by week 12, your timeline may differ.

#2: Anti-inflammatory diet (90 days strict)

Eliminated: all dairy (IGF-1 → oil), gluten, sugar, processed food, inflammatory seed oils.

Added: greens + moringa smoothie daily, salmon 3×/week, fiber (sweet potato, quinoa, legumes), bone broth, ginger/turmeric.

#3: Stress management

Morning walk, 10-min meditation, work boundaries, bed by 10:30pm, magnesium 300mg. High cortisol = more androgens = more acne.

#4: Targeted supplements (~$96/month)

  • Morning: 2 tsp NutriThrive moringa ($11/mo)
  • Lunch: probiotic + omega-3
  • Dinner: zinc 30mg + evening primrose oil
  • Bed: magnesium glycinate

Cost was roughly ~$100/month in food supplements and probiotics, compare with whatever your dermatologist recommends for your situation.

The Foods That DESTROYED My Skin

#1 Dairy (worst)

Glass of milk → 3 cysts in 48 hours. Raises IGF-1 and insulin. Now: zero dairy; almond/oat milk only.

#2 Sugar

Chocolate, soft drink, "healthy" granola, spikes insulin, feeds bad bacteria. Now: <25g/day.

#3 Gluten

Bread, pasta, pizza, bloating + breakouts if sensitive.

#4 Vegetable oils

Canola, soybean in restaurant/packaged food, pro-inflammatory omega-6.

#5 Alcohol

Wine/beer → cysts 2 days later. Rare clear spirits only.

Your 90-Day Skin Diary Plan

Weeks 1 to 4: Gut reset

Eliminate: dairy, gluten, sugar, alcohol, processed food.

Add: 2 tsp moringa daily, probiotic 50B, omega-3 2000mg, bone broth, sauerkraut.

Expect: possible detox breakout weeks 1 to 2; calmer skin by week 3 to 4; better digestion.

Weeks 5 to 8: Rebuild

Continue elimination. Add zinc, evening primrose, fermented foods, veg every meal, berries only for fruit.

Skincare: gentle cleanser, simple moisturizer, SPF 50, don't pick.

What I noticed: fewer new breakouts for me; cysts slowly healing, your timeline may differ.

Weeks 9 to 12: Reintroduce & test

One food every 3 days: quinoa/rice → low-sugar fruit → eggs → gluten → dairy last. Breakout within 3 days = eliminate.

What I hoped for: gradual improvement, not a guaranteed "clear" outcome.

Month 4+: Maintenance (80/20)

Non-negotiables: no dairy, limited sugar, daily moringa + probiotic, sleep, stress care. Simple skincare + retinol 2 to 3×/week.

FAQs

Will this work for cystic acne?

I had 15 to 20 active cysts. For me, weeks 1 to 4 were slow (gut adjustment), 5 to 8 showed fewer new breakouts, 9 to 12 was when I saw the biggest change. Be patient, and stay in touch with a dermatologist if acne is severe.

Do I really need to cut dairy?

For most hormonal acne: yes. Cut 30 days strict, reintroduce 1 serving, watch 48 to 72 hours. If you break out, it's a trigger.

Probiotics alone without diet?

No. Sugar, dairy, and processed food undo probiotics. Remove triggers + add healers (probiotics, moringa, real food).

How long until results?

Weeks 2 to 3: digestion · 4 to 6: fewer new breakouts for me · 8 to 12: gradual healing · months 4 to 6: maintenance. Weeks 1 to 4 may "purge" for some, see a dermatologist if it worsens.

Why moringa for acne?

Anti-inflammatory (quercetin, chlorogenic acid), prebiotic fiber, vitamin A for skin turnover, zinc for oil control, ~$11/month. NutriThrive Moringa

What about topicals?

Use simple support only: AM water rinse + moisturizer + SPF; PM gentle cleanser + retinol 2×/week + moisturizer. 80% internal, 20% topical.

Will acne come back?

If you return to dairy, sugar, no probiotics, yes. Maintenance: 80/20 diet, daily moringa + probiotic, stress and sleep. Lifestyle, not a quick fix.

The Harsh Truth About Acne

  1. Dermatologists often treat symptoms, not gut/root causes.
  2. It's not your fault, modern antibiotics, BC, processed food damage the microbiome.
  3. Quick fixes (LED masks, $85 serums) rarely work long-term.
  4. Healing takes 12 to 24 weeks minimum.
  5. Food is medicine, dairy and sugar are common triggers.
