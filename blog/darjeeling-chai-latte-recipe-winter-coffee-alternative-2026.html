Darjeeling Chai Latte Recipe: The Winter Coffee Alternative (2026)

By Neer, NutriThrive Truganina · Last updated: 28 Jul 2026

Quick Answer

Steep 1.5 tsp Darjeeling in 100ml water at about 90°C for 3 minutes. Warm oat milk with cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, and ginger. Strain tea, strain spiced milk over it, stir in honey. About 7 minutes, half the caffeine of coffee.

Key Takeaways
  • Darjeeling chai tastes more like tea than standard Assam-based cafe chai.
  • Roughly 40 to 50mg caffeine per mug: about half a coffee.
  • Home cost is around $0.60 per cup versus $7 to $9 at a cafe.
  • Make a spiced milk concentrate for 3 days; brew Darjeeling fresh each morning.
  • Steep exactly 3 minutes: longer gets bitter.

Why Darjeeling works here where Assam doesn't

Most Australians who want to reduce coffee stop trying because the alternatives do not feel like an upgrade. Herbal tea is pleasant but not the same. Green tea is fine but not warming enough for winter mornings.

Darjeeling chai latte is different. It is warming, spiced, has enough caffeine to start the morning (about half a coffee), and with good Darjeeling it beats most $8 cafe chai lattes. It takes about 7 minutes.

Standard chai uses Assam or CTC tea: strong and malty, built to punch through milk and spice. Darjeeling's muscatel and floral notes complement cardamom and cinnamon instead of disappearing under them. The result tastes more like tea and less like spiced milk with brown colour.

The Recipe: Darjeeling Chai Latte

Makes: 1 large mug
Time: 7 minutes

Ingredients

  • 1.5 tsp NutriThrive Darjeeling loose leaf tea
  • 200ml oat milk (or regular whole milk)
  • 100ml water
  • 1 cinnamon stick (or ¼ tsp ground cinnamon)
  • 3 cardamom pods, lightly crushed
  • 2 cloves
  • 1 thin slice fresh ginger
  • 1 tsp honey (or to taste)

Method

Step 1 : Brew the Darjeeling. Let boiled water sit 60 seconds (about 90°C). Pour 100ml over 1.5 tsp Darjeeling. Steep exactly 3 minutes. Strain into your mug.

Step 2 : Make spiced milk. While tea steeps, warm oat milk with cinnamon, cardamom, cloves, and ginger over medium-low heat for 3 to 4 minutes. Steam, do not boil.

Step 3 : Combine. Strain warm spiced milk over the Darjeeling. Stir in honey.

Step 4 : Optional. Froth the milk before straining for a cafe-style texture.

Why make this at home rather than buy it

Cafe chai latte: $7 to $9, often made with syrup concentrate and instant tea powder. This at home: about $0.60 per cup using NutriThrive Darjeeling. Better taste, whole spices, less sugar.

Over a month of daily use: roughly $18 at home versus $210 at the cafe.

Variations: Stronger chai: 2 tsp Darjeeling, steep 4 minutes. Iced: brew stronger, pour over ice, add cold frothed oat milk. Lower-caffeine evening version: steep 30 seconds, discard, re-steep 3 minutes (most caffeine extracts early).

FAQ

What makes Darjeeling chai different from regular chai latte?

Most chai lattes use strong Assam or CTC tea: bold, malty, and designed to be masked by spice and milk. Darjeeling brings muscatel and floral notes that work with the chai spices rather than disappearing under them, giving a more complex, less bitter base.

How much caffeine is in a Darjeeling chai latte?

Roughly 40 to 50mg, about half a standard coffee. The spices add no caffeine. A good choice if you want to reduce coffee without going caffeine-free.

Can I make a large batch of Darjeeling chai?

Yes. Make a spiced milk concentrate by simmering milk with spices for 10 minutes and refrigerating. Each morning, brew fresh Darjeeling and add warm concentrate. Keeps 3 days in the fridge.
