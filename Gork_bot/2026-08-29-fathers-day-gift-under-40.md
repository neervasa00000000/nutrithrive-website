## How to use each item (one paragraph each)

**Moringa, 100 g.** Leaf powder for food. Start at half a teaspoon in something he already eats: eggs, yoghurt, a smoothie, dal, soup. After a week, most adults who tolerate it use about **1 teaspoon (roughly 2–3 g)** once or twice a day with food. Tastes like dried spinach, not a flavoured shake. 100 g is a month-plus at a teaspoon a day, not a lifetime supply. A heaped dessert spoon is a stomach problem, not a shortcut. Blood thinners, pregnancy, breastfeeding, or prescription medicine: GP first. [FAQ](https://nutrithrive.com.au/faq) and [lab summary](https://nutrithrive.com.au/documents/nutrithrive-lab-report-summary.pdf).

**Darjeeling, 100 g.** Loose leaf. **1–2 teaspoons per cup**. Water at **85–90°C**, not a rolling boil. Steep **3–5 minutes**. Boiling water makes it harsh. Plain, or a splash of milk. About **40–50 mg caffeine per cup** on our FAQ: black-tea amount, not coffee. 100 g is roughly **50 cups** at 2 teaspoons per pot. If he only drinks English Breakfast bags, this will taste lighter and more floral. That is Darjeeling, not a defect.

**Dried curry leaves, 30 g.** For the pan, not a salad. Dried leaf is quieter than fresh, so use about **two to three times** the fresh amount. Heat oil or ghee, throw the leaves in at the start (tadka), let them crackle, then add onion or mustard seed. Dal, sambar, fried fish, potatoes, scrambled eggs. 30 g is a lot of tadkas. Store airtight, dark, dry. They are not curry powder. Starting recipe: [30-minute curry leaf dal](https://nutrithrive.com.au/blog/curry-leaves-dahl-recipe-30-minutes-australia-2026).

**Moringa lavender soap, 95 g.** It is a bar. Wet skin, lather, rinse, leave it on a draining dish. Daily use typically lasts **4–6 weeks** if it is not sitting in the shower stream. Not a treatment, not a face-clinic product, not a cure. Handmade in Australia, 95 g net. Comparison: [Moringa soap vs regular soap](https://nutrithrive.com.au/blog/moringa-soap-vs-regular-soap-comparison-2026). Gift it as a bar of soap. That is the whole claim.
