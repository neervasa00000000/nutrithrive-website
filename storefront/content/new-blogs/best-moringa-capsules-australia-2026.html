How to compare capsules, in 4 steps

1. Leaf per capsule. The pack should state it, for example 500mg per capsule. If it only says "extract" with no leaf equivalent, you cannot compare it with leaf powder. 2. Total leaf. Capsule count times leaf per capsule: 120 at 500mg is 60g. 3. Price per 100g. Total price, including shipping, divided by grams, times 100. 4. Drying and listing. Freeze-dried is dried at low temperature. Certified organic means an independent certifier checked the farm. An ARTG number means it is listed as a complementary medicine in Australia.

What to check before you buy

Leaf weight per capsule; total leaf in the bottle; price per 100g delivered; ingredients, with no fillers unless clearly labelled; drying method; certified organic and ARTG number, if listed; seller, shipping origin, and returns; and the best-before date. If a listing cannot answer those points, compare it with one that can.

Capsules suit you if you dislike the taste of green powder, if you travel a lot, if you want a fixed dose without measuring, or if you take other capsules already.
