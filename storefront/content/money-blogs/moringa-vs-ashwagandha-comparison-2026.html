What moringa actually is

Moringa leaf powder is ground, dried leaf, used in drinks or meals. NutriThrive sells leaf powder only. We grow the leaf on our family farm, sun-dry it and pack it in Truganina. Check the ingredient and pack information rather than assuming all products are equivalent.

What ashwagandha actually is

Ashwagandha is a different plant, Withania somnifera. Products can contain root, leaf or extracts of both. An extract label and a food-powder label describe different products; their gram weights do not establish equivalent servings or effects.

Compare the format first

  • Moringa leaf powder: a loose powder you mix into food; it has a noticeable green flavour.
  • Ashwagandha preparations: often capsules or extracts. Read the ingredient, concentration and directions on the particular pack.
  • Buying either: check the seller, batch information, label and testing documents.

Food information is not a medical recommendation

Do not use this comparison to diagnose a nutrient deficiency, choose a treatment or replace prescribed care. If you are concerned about symptoms or your diet, talk to your GP before choosing a supplement.

Check with your GP first

The NCCIH safety overview advises avoiding ashwagandha during pregnancy and breastfeeding and flags possible medicine interactions. Bring the actual product label to your GP or pharmacist, especially if you take medicines or have a health condition.

Can you take both?

This page does not establish that combining them is suitable for you. Check the proposed combination and product labels with your GP or pharmacist first.

FAQ

Are moringa and ashwagandha interchangeable?

No. They are different plants, and leaf powder is not equivalent to a concentrated herbal extract.

Does NutriThrive sell ashwagandha?

No. NutriThrive sells moringa leaf powder, not ashwagandha products.
