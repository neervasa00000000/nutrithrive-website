Diwali hamper buying checklist

Run through this before you pay for any hamper, ours or anyone else's:

  1. A clear contents list. You should see every item and its size (for example "Darjeeling tea 100g"), not just "assorted goodies".
  2. Keep time. Fresh sweets need to be eaten quickly. If the hamper is going interstate or the recipient is travelling over Diwali, choose something that keeps.
  3. Delivery area. Some Diwali hamper sellers deliver only within Sydney or Melbourne for the festival. Check that the recipient's postcode is covered before you choose a design.
  4. Order-by date. Look for a stated cut-off for Diwali, not just "ships in 2 to 5 days". Rural addresses take longer.
  5. Total cost with delivery. Compare the price at checkout, not the price on the tile. A $40 basket with $20 delivery costs more than a $20 box with standard postage.
  6. Dietary notes. If sweets are included, check for egg, nuts, gluten and dairy. For a household with mixed diets, non-food or pantry items are simpler.
  7. Gift note and packing. Can you add a message? Is anything scented packed apart from the food? (We wrap our soap separately so the tea doesn't take on its scent.)
  8. Returns. Know the policy before you buy. Ours is seven days on unopened products.
