Quick answer

  • AG1: branded multi-ingredient daily scoop (check the current AG1 label for the full list).
  • NutriThrive: one ingredient, moringa leaf powder. Not an AG1 clone. Not certified organic.
  • Shop: moringa powder, sizes and prices on that page.

What AG1 is

AG1 markets itself as an all-in-one greens and nutrient scoop. The formula is a blend: many plants, vitamins or minerals depending on the current label, and a scoop habit aimed at a daily drink. Confirm ingredients and price on AG1’s Australian checkout before you buy, we do not republish their full formula here because it can change.

What we sell

Farm-grown Moringa oleifera leaf powder only. Stir into smoothies, yoghurt, dal or tea. See how to choose moringa powder, moringa tea, and powder vs capsules.
