Three green powders show up in the same shopping basket: moringa, spirulina and matcha. They taste different and fit different habits. Here’s a side-by-side so you can pick a format, then buy NutriThrive only if single-ingredient moringa leaf powder is what you want.

Quick answer

  • Moringa: dried leaf powder; grassy; food and drinks.
  • Spirulina: algae powder; stronger marine/earthy note.
  • Matcha: stone-ground green tea; bitter-umami; caffeine.
  • We sell moringa only: shop powder.

Moringa works in savoury food as well as drinks. Spirulina is usually a scoop-in-smoothie habit. Matcha is a tea ritual with caffeine. Smoothie ideas for leaf powder: moringa smoothie recipes. Tea: moringa tea guide.
