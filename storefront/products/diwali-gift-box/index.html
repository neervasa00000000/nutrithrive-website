What's in the box

  • Darjeeling loose leaf black tea, 100g: about 50 cups. Floral, muscatel notes at 85–90°C. Contains caffeine (about 40–50mg a cup).
  • Dried curry leaves, 30g: whole leaves for tadka. Use two to three times the fresh amount.
  • Handmade moringa & lavender soap, 95g: melt-and-pour base, moringa leaf, lavender fragrance, dried lavender flowers.

Who it's for: relatives you're visiting, a host who has plenty of mithai, neighbours, teachers and colleagues. For bulk orders, contact us.
