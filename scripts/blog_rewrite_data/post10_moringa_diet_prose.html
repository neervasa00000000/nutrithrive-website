Food vehicles that work

Smoothie: Hide the grassy note with banana, berries, or oat milk. Start at ½ tsp. Recipes: moringa smoothie recipes (Australia).

Yoghurt or oats: Stir ½ tsp into thick yoghurt or overnight oats with honey or fruit.

Savoury breakfast: Mash ½ tsp into avocado toast with lemon and salt. See moringa avocado toast.

Soup, dal, eggs: Stir ½ tsp into a hot bowl off the boil so it mixes without scorching.

Small drink: For a tiny glass rather than a meal, see our moringa shot recipe (homemade mix — we do not sell a shot product).

With milk or water

Yes, you can mix moringa with milk. Warm (not boiling) milk with a little honey is easier than cold milk alone. With water only, add lemon or ginger; plain water is the hardest taste test. Whisk well so you do not leave dry clumps.

Taste and common mistakes

  • Starting with a full tablespoon
  • Only ever mixing into plain cold water
  • Buying capsules when you wanted powder for food
  • Skipping fat or acid (yoghurt, milk, lemon) that softens the leaf note
