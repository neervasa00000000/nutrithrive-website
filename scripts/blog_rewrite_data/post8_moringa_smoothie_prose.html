How much moringa powder in a smoothie?

Start with ½ teaspoon, especially if you are new to the taste. Cap at 1 level teaspoon per serve. That is a normal food amount, not a therapeutic dose.

Golden rule: ½–1 tsp maximum per smoothie. A heaped tablespoon rarely tastes good.

1. Mango banana (the easiest)

Moringa powder: ½ tsp to start, up to 1 tsp maximum.

Ingredients: 1 frozen banana · 1 cup frozen mango · 1 cup coconut water · ½ tsp moringa powder

Why it works: Frozen mango masks the hay-like leaf note better than almost any other fruit. Good first recipe if you have been sceptical.

2. Peanut butter chocolate

Moringa powder: ½ tsp to start, up to 1 tsp maximum.

Ingredients: 1 frozen banana · 1 tbsp natural peanut butter · 1 tbsp cacao powder · 1 cup oat milk · ½ tsp moringa powder

Why it works: Cacao and peanut butter are strong enough to balance the leaf. The colour goes brown-green, but the flavour holds up.

3. Pineapple ginger lime

Moringa powder: ½ tsp to start, up to 1 tsp maximum.

Ingredients: 1 cup frozen pineapple · ½ cup cucumber · juice of 1 lime · thumb of fresh ginger · 1 cup water · ½ tsp moringa powder

Why it works: Acid and ginger shift the profile from grassy to sharp and bright. No banana required.

4. Avocado mint

Moringa powder: ½ tsp to start, up to 1 tsp maximum.

Ingredients: ½ avocado · handful fresh mint · 1 frozen banana · 1 cup almond milk · ½ tsp moringa powder

Why it works: Mint competes with the leaf; avocado adds creaminess. This is the bright-green smoothie people expect from photos.

5. Tropical protein

Moringa powder: ½ tsp to start, up to 1 tsp maximum.

Ingredients: ½ cup frozen mango · ½ cup frozen pineapple · 1 cup coconut milk · 2 tbsp natural yoghurt · ½ tsp moringa powder

Why it works: Yoghurt adds tang and body. Filling enough for breakfast when you want food, not just a green drink.

Taste fixes that actually work

  1. Too bitter or grassy: add half a ripe banana or one tablespoon peanut butter.
  2. Too thin: add half a frozen banana or two tablespoons yoghurt and blend again.
  3. Too sweet: halve the fruit, add ice, keep moringa at ½ tsp.

FAQ

How much moringa powder do I put in a smoothie?

½ teaspoon to 1 level teaspoon per serve. Start low; more than 1 tsp usually tastes too grassy.

What fruit hides the taste of moringa best?

Frozen mango and ripe banana are the most effective. Pineapple works well too.

Can I put moringa in a smoothie every day?

Many people stir ½–1 tsp into food or drinks daily as a kitchen habit. This is food use only; follow your own comfort and any advice from your health professional.
