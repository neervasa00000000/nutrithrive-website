How to compare moringa brands in Australia

Online brands, pharmacy listings, and bulk bags all use “premium” language. A facts-only check means writing down what you can verify on the pack or website before you trust a ranking. This page is not a Chemist Warehouse aisle tour, not a capsules essay, and not the full eight-point quality checklist — that lives on its own page.

The five-point brand checklist

  1. Lab paperwork: can you open a certificate or summary that shows results for the lot you might buy?
  2. Single-ingredient label: is the list 100% moringa leaf without unnamed blends?
  3. Processing: does the seller say shade-dried? Bright green powder is a common clue; dull brown can mean harsh drying or age.
  4. Pack date: is there a pack or best-before date you can read?
  5. Price per 100g: divide shelf price by grams, multiply by 100 — so 500g and 100g pouches are comparable.
