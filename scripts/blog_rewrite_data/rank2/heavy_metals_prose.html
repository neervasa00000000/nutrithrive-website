Why leaf powder gets tested

Plants take up minerals from soil and water. Testing does not prove a brand is “best”; it shows whether a named lot was checked against stated limits. Prefer documents that name the product, lot or batch, analytes and lab.

What a useful CoA shows

  • Product identity and lot/batch ID
  • Lab name and test date
  • Heavy metals panel (for example lead, arsenic, cadmium, mercury) with units and limits
  • Optional: pesticides or microbiology if the seller claims them

Marketing PDFs without a lot ID are weaker than batch-linked reports.

Organic ≠ metal-free

Certified organic rules cover how crops are grown and processed. They are not a substitute for metals testing. See organic vs farm-grown.

What should I ask a moringa seller about heavy metals?

Ask for a recent, batch-linked CoA or lab summary that lists heavy metals with units and limits.

Does organic certification replace metals testing?

No. Ask for both if both matter to you.
