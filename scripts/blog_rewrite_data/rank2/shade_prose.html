Drying method shows up in how sellers describe leaf powder. “Shade-dried” and “sun-dried” are process words — use them as questions for any brand, including us, then confirm pack facts and lab paperwork before you buy.

Quick answer

  • Shade-dried usually means leaf dried out of direct harsh sun; ask the seller how they dry and mill.
  • Sun-dried leaf can still be food-grade — read the label and paperwork either way.
  • NutriThrive shade-dries leaf powder and packs in Truganina. Shop powder.

What shade-dried means here

On our farm we dry leaf under shade conditions before milling into powder. The goal is a bright, food-use leaf powder without claiming medical outcomes. Colour and aroma still vary by harvest — open the pouch and use your senses plus the lab summary.

What to ask any seller

  • Is the product leaf only?
  • How was it dried (shade, sun, mechanical)?
  • Where was it packed, and can you see a batch or lab summary?
  • What is the dated price per 100g?
