Shipping and returns across Australia

Orders leave Truganina. Free on Australian orders of $49 or more. Under $49 in Australia, shipping is by parcel weight.

Australia standard shipping for orders under $49, by parcel weight: up to 250 g $9.69 · 251 to 500 g $11.12 · 501 g to 1 kg $15.20 · 1 to 3 kg $19.24 · 3 to 5 kg $23.23.

Dispatch

Order before 2pm Monday to Friday for same-day Melbourne dispatch. Free shipping is applied automatically in the cart once your Australian order reaches $49. International postage is calculated from the parcel weight.

Timing after dispatch

Melbourne metro usually 1 to 3 business days. Other metro areas usually 3 to 4 business days. Rural and regional areas up to 10 business days.

Tracking

When the parcel leaves Truganina you should get a confirmation email with tracking, if the carrier provides it. If nothing arrives within a couple of business days after dispatch, check spam or email us with your order details.

7-day guarantee

Contact us within 7 days of delivery and we'll refund or replace your order. If something arrived damaged or faulty, send us a photo of the item and packaging, and we'll sort it at no cost to you. Change-of-mind returns must be unopened, and you pay the return postage. Original shipping isn't refunded. Please contact us before you send anything back.

Payments

Online: PayPal or card. Phone / Truganina pickup by arrangement: bank transfer, PayID or cash.

Shipping FAQ

Free Australia-wide shipping on orders of $49 or more. Free shipping is applied automatically in the cart. Under $49 in Australia, standard rates start from $9.69 by parcel weight. International postage is calculated from the parcel weight.

Online 24/7. Local pickup from Truganina by arrangement. See our Melbourne page.
