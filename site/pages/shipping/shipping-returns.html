Track your order

When your parcel leaves Truganina, we'll email your tracking details if the carrier provides them. If you haven't received an email within a couple of business days, check spam or contact us with your order details.

7-day guarantee

Contact us within 7 days of delivery and we'll refund or replace your order. If something arrived damaged or faulty, send us a photo of the item and packaging, and we'll sort it at no cost to you.

Change-of-mind returns must be unopened, and you pay the return postage. Original shipping isn't refunded. Please contact us before you send anything back.

Shipping FAQ

Where do you dispatch orders from?

We pack and dispatch from Truganina, Melbourne. Australia-wide tracked shipping.

How do I get free shipping?

Free Australia-wide shipping on orders of $49 or more. Free shipping is applied automatically in the cart. Under $49 in Australia, standard rates start from $9.69 by parcel weight. International postage is calculated from the parcel weight.

Can I buy / pick up in Melbourne?

Online 24/7. Local pickup from Truganina by arrangement. See our Melbourne page.
