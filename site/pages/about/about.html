Our farm in Gujarat

I'm Neer Vasa, and I started NutriThrive in 2023. Our moringa and curry leaves are grown on our family farm in Gujarat, India.

The leaves are picked on the farm and sun-dried to keep their colour. The moringa is then ground into powder, and everything is packed by me in Truganina, Melbourne, before it's posted to you.

Quality should not be out of reach

NutriThrive moringa starts at $11 per 100g. Selling direct is how we keep it at that price.

Testing and transparency

Our moringa powder is tested in Australia and we publish the available readable summary. It shows the nutrition results for batch NT042024, tested by the National Measurement Institute in January 2025, so you can see exactly what's in it.
