Local time and postage

Ordering from Brisbane

Order before 2pm Monday to Friday, Melbourne time, for same-day dispatch. Queensland has no daylight saving, so that's 1pm in Brisbane from October to April and 2pm the rest of the year. Brisbane does not move its clock when Melbourne does. From October to April, use 1pm Brisbane time. For the rest of the year, use 2pm Brisbane time.

Postage is by weight and costs the same to Brisbane as anywhere in Australia: $9.69 up to 250g, $11.12 for 251–500g, free from $49. A 100g pouch is $20.69 delivered, 200g is $31.69 and 400g is $51.12. Add a 100g pouch to a 400g ($51 total) and shipping is free.

Regional and rural QLD addresses can take up to 10 business days.
