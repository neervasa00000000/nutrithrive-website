Why pantry gifts beat leftover mithai

By the second week of the festival most homes already have more barfi and ladoo than anyone can finish. A pouch of dried curry leaves or a pouch of Darjeeling disappears into real routines (tadka for dal, a mid-afternoon cup) instead of waiting for a special occasion that never comes. Pantry staples also travel well and don't melt in warm weather.
