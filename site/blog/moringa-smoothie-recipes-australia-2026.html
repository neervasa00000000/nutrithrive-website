Quick answer

Can I add moringa powder to my smoothie? Yes. Start with ½ tsp (about 1g) and go up to 1 level tsp (about 2g) per smoothie. More than that and the grassy note usually wins. Sizes: 100g $11 · 200g $21.50 · 400g bundle $35. Shop moringa powder, shipped Australia-wide.

How much moringa powder in a smoothie?

Start with ½ teaspoon (about 1g), especially if the taste is new to you. Cap it at 1 level teaspoon (about 2g) per serve. That is an ordinary food amount, not a therapeutic dose.

Golden rule: ½ to 1 tsp maximum per smoothie. A heaped tablespoon rarely tastes good.

How long a pouch lasts at 1 tsp a day: 100g ≈ 50 smoothies · 200g ≈ 100 · 400g ≈ 200.

Where to buy the ingredients in Australia

Coconut water, oat milk and almond milk are in the long-life milk aisle. Cacao is in the baking or health food aisle. Ranges vary by store, so check your local one. What's in season in Australia

  • Summer (Dec–Feb): mango (Kensington Pride, Calypso, R2E2), passionfruit, stone fruit, watermelon. This is the best time for recipes 1 and 5.
  • Autumn (Mar–May): banana, apple, pear, passionfruit, feijoa.
  • Winter (Jun–Aug): citrus (navel orange, mandarin, lime), kiwifruit. Use frozen mango or pineapple.
  • Spring (Sep–Nov): strawberries, early mango from the north, Queensland pineapple.

Tip: buy mangoes cheaply in peak summer, then dice and freeze them for winter smoothies.

1. Mango banana (the easiest)

Moringa powder: ½ tsp to start, up to 1 tsp maximum.

Ingredients: 1 frozen banana · 1 cup (about 150g) frozen mango · 1 cup (250ml) coconut water · ½ tsp moringa powder

Why it works: frozen mango masks the hay-like leaf note better than almost any other fruit. It's a good first recipe if you've been sceptical.

AU swap: in summer, use fresh Kensington Pride or Calypso mango plus 3 ice cubes.

2. Peanut butter chocolate

Moringa powder: ½ tsp to start, up to 1 tsp maximum.

Ingredients: 1 frozen banana · 1 tbsp (20g) natural peanut butter · 1 tbsp (about 8g) cacao powder · 1 cup (250ml) oat milk · ½ tsp moringa powder

Why it works: cacao and peanut butter are strong enough to balance the leaf. The colour goes brown-green, but the flavour holds up. This works as a moringa powder shake too: add a scoop of your usual protein powder.

3. Pineapple ginger lime

Moringa powder: ½ tsp to start, up to 1 tsp maximum.

Ingredients: 1 cup (about 160g) frozen pineapple · ½ cup (about 65g) cucumber · juice of 1 lime · thumb (about 2cm) of fresh ginger · 1 cup (250ml) water · ½ tsp moringa powder

Why it works: acid and ginger shift the profile from grassy to sharp and bright. No banana needed.

AU swap: in winter, swap the lime for the juice of 1 navel orange.

4. Avocado mint

Moringa powder: ½ tsp to start, up to 1 tsp maximum.

Ingredients: ½ avocado (Hass or Shepard) · handful fresh mint · 1 frozen banana · 1 cup (250ml) almond milk · ½ tsp moringa powder

Why it works: mint competes with the leaf and avocado adds creaminess. This is the bright-green smoothie people expect from photos.

5. Tropical protein

Moringa powder: ½ tsp to start, up to 1 tsp maximum.

Ingredients: ½ cup (about 75g) frozen mango · ½ cup (about 80g) frozen pineapple · 1 cup (250ml) coconut milk · 2 tbsp (about 40g) natural or Greek yoghurt · ½ tsp moringa powder

Why it works: yoghurt adds tang and body. It's filling enough for breakfast when you want food, not just a green drink.

AU swap: in summer, stir in the pulp of 1 passionfruit after blending.

Bonus: passionfruit mango

1 cup (150g) frozen mango · pulp of 2 passionfruit · 1 cup (250ml) coconut water · ½ tsp moringa powder. Blend everything except the passionfruit, then stir the pulp through so the seeds stay whole.

Taste fixes that actually work

  • Too bitter or grassy: add half a ripe banana or 1 tablespoon of peanut butter.
  • Too thin: add half a frozen banana or 2 tablespoons of yoghurt and blend again.
  • Too sweet: halve the fruit, add ice, and keep moringa at ½ tsp.

Make-ahead vs blend fresh

Bag the fruit the night before and add moringa only when you blend, so the powder stays fresh. FAQ

How much moringa powder do I put in a smoothie?

½ teaspoon (about 1g) to 1 level teaspoon (about 2g) per serve. Start low; more than 1 tsp usually tastes too grassy.

What fruit hides the taste of moringa best?

Frozen mango and ripe banana work best. Pineapple works well too, and so does passionfruit in summer.

Can I put moringa in a smoothie every day?

Many people stir ½ to 1 tsp into food or drinks daily as a kitchen habit. This is food use only, so go by your own comfort and any advice from your health professional.

Is NutriThrive moringa organic?

No. We grow moringa on our own farm and describe it as farm-grown and shade-dried. We are not certified organic.

Where can I buy moringa powder in Australia?

Online from NutriThrive. We pack it in Truganina, Melbourne, and ship it Australia-wide.

How much does moringa powder cost in Australia?

Do Woolworths or Coles sell moringa powder?

Range varies by store and changes over time. All the other smoothie ingredients on this page are standard supermarket items.

Is malunggay powder the same as moringa powder?

Yes. Malunggay is the Filipino name for moringa leaf, so you can use it in these recipes the same way.

Can I make a moringa shake instead of a smoothie?

Yes. Blend ½ tsp moringa with 250ml milk, 1 frozen banana and your usual protein powder (recipe 2 works well).

Can kids have moringa smoothies?

Keep to small food amounts, like a pinch to ½ tsp blended into fruit. Check with your health professional if you're unsure.
