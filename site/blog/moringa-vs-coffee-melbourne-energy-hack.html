Does moringa have caffeine?

Quick answer: No. Moringa leaf powder is not a caffeine product. Every milligram of caffeine in your morning cup comes from the coffee, tea or matcha, not from the leaf.

That is the whole answer for the leaf itself. The rest of this page covers the question many Melbourne readers are asking this spring: what a cup actually costs now.

Does moringa tea have caffeine?

Plain moringa leaf has no caffeine. A blend that also contains green tea or black tea does, because the tea part has caffeine. Read the label before you assume a "moringa tea" bag is only leaf.

Can you put moringa powder in coffee?

We get this question a lot, and our answer is deliberately short: we don't publish mixing, serving or recipe directions for moringa leaf powder. What we can say is factual. Plain leaf powder adds no caffeine, so any caffeine in the cup comes from the coffee. If you're looking at a ready-made "moringa coffee" blend, read the label, because many of them also contain coffee, tea or added sugar.

Why your cafe coffee costs more this spring

From 1 October 2026, cafes can no longer add a surcharge for paying by card. Small Flat White re-priced 3,309 cafes against their own September price and found that 20% have put their flat white price up, with a typical rise of 50 cents (checked 27 October 2026). The ABC reported the same pattern in its 7 October story on cafe prices.

That lands on top of a tight household budget. The Reserve Bank lifted the cash rate to 4.60% on 29 September 2026, and the ABS found household spending was flat in August and would have fallen 0.3% without fuel. None of that means giving up your cafe coffee. It does make the second or third cup of the day worth a second look.
