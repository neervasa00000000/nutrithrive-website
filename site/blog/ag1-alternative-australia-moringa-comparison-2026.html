Quick answer

Moringa powder is not a one-for-one AG1 replacement. AG1 is a branded blend of many ingredients; our product is one shade-dried leaf. Compare them on what you want from a daily scoop and what you are willing to pay, not on matching label lines.

AG1 (Athletic Greens) built a category: one morning scoop, dozens of add-ins, subscription delivery. Australians google AG1 alternative Australia, AG1 equivalent Australia, and AG1 Australia price when that subscription stings or when they want something simpler. Fair enough. We are a Melbourne farmer-manufacturer selling plain moringa powder. We do not sell an AG1 clone and we will not pretend our pouch replicates AG1’s full panel.

Who this page is for

You want honest context, not a trophy chart. You might like the ritual of mixing greens into water or a smoothie, but you are unsure whether you need probiotics, adaptogens, and twelve other powders in the same tub.

This page is less useful if you specifically want AG1’s all-in-one stack unchanged. Buy AG1 from the brand, or pick another multi-formula product. Our job is to explain where a single leaf powder fits instead.

Is there an AG1 equivalent in Australia?

No perfect equivalent. AG1 equivalent Australia searches usually mean “cheaper local option.” Chemist Warehouse and other retailers stock various greens blends; AG1 itself is mostly DTC. None of those products is nutritionally identical to AG1, and neither is moringa alone.

A fair comparison is purpose: broad formula versus one ingredient you can read on the bag and cross-check with a lab report. If your goal is a simple leaf habit with transparent sourcing, moringa can work. If your goal is maximal ingredient count in one scoop, stick with a stacked blend.

What is in AG1 (and what we sell instead)

AG1 is a proprietary multi-ingredient greens powder: vitamins, minerals, plant extracts, probiotics, enzymes, and a long list of fruit and vegetable powders. Exact per-ingredient weights are not public in the way a single-ingredient product is. Check AG1’s current Australian label for the live list.

NutriThrive moringa is one ingredient: shade-dried moringa leaf, tested and packed in Truganina. Sizes: 100g $11, 200g $21.50, 400g $35. Free AU shipping from $79.

Does AG1 have moringa?

Does AG1 have moringa? AG1’s greens base has commonly included moringa among many other ingredients. It is a small part of a large blend, not the whole product. Our pouch is the opposite: 100% leaf, no proprietary mix.

If you landed here because you noticed moringa on AG1’s label and wondered about buying leaf on its own, that is exactly the split this page describes.

Where plain moringa fits as a moringa alternative

Strip the marketing back and many people mainly want a consistent plant powder to round out breakfast. Moringa covers that narrower job:

  • One ingredient. You know what is in the bag and where it was grown.
  • Routine-friendly. Stir into water, juice, or a smoothie without a subscription lock-in.
  • Price clarity. Published pouch prices, not a moving AG1 subscription quote.
  • Lab context. We publish testing context on the product page; you are not guessing at a proprietary blend.

That is what people often mean by a moringa alternative to a greens scoop: simpler, not “the same product cheaper.”

Where AG1 still does more

AG1 includes categories moringa does not: added probiotics, digestive enzymes, mushroom extracts, and a wider spread of powdered plants. If you want all of that in one tub, a leaf powder will not replicate it. We would rather say that plainly than oversell a $11 pouch as a full formula substitute.

Simple leaf vs stacked formula

Choose a stacked formula like AG1 if you want probiotics, enzymes, and the widest ingredient list in one scoop and you are happy paying AG1’s current Australian price for that breadth.

There is no perfect AG1 equivalent Australia. Multi-greens tubs at pharmacies and online stores are different formulas. Moringa is one leaf, not a clone.

Does AG1 have moringa?

Often yes, as one line in a long blend. NutriThrive sells moringa only, with published sizes and prices on the product page.

What does AG1 cost in Australia?

AG1 Australia price varies by subscription and offers. Confirm on AG1’s site. Our powder starts at $11 for 100g with free shipping from $79.

Why is AG1 expensive?

You are paying for a multi-ingredient formula, brand, and fulfilment, not just dried leaf by the kilo. That is normal for heavily marketed supplement blends.

No. We sell shade-dried moringa leaf powder grown on our farm, nothing else in the bag.
