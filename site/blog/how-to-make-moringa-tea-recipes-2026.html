Quick answer

Use about ½ teaspoon farm-grown moringa powder per cup. Make a paste first with a splash of water or milk, then top up with liquid around 70 to 80°C, not a rolling boil on the powder. Finish with lemon, ginger, honey, or milk. Food-use only; this is a drink recipe, not medical advice.

Moringa “tea” here means leaf powder stirred into hot or cold water, not a bag of black tea with moringa flavour. The usual mistake is pouring boiling kettle water straight onto the powder, the cup turns harsh and people assume they dislike moringa. Why not pour boiling water on the powder?

Water at 100°C pulls bitter notes forward and can make the cup taste muddy. Let the kettle sit 2 to 3 minutes after boiling (roughly 70 to 80°C), or add a splash of cold water before you stir in moringa. You are making a food drink, not brewing tannin-heavy tea leaves.

No-boil paste method

Whisk ½ tsp moringa with a little room-temperature water until smooth, then top up with cool or lukewarm water. Same paste trick works for iced drinks: paste, cold water, chill, pour over ice. Paste-first mixing stops green clumps floating on top.

Recipe 1: Basic moringa tea with lemon

You need: ½ tsp moringa powder, 250ml water at ~75°C (or cool water + paste method), juice of ½ lemon, honey to taste.

  1. Stir powder with a splash of water to a paste (10 to 15 seconds).
  2. Add remaining warm water; whisk 20 to 30 seconds total.
  3. Add lemon and honey. Drink warm or chilled over ice.

Recipe 2: Moringa ginger honey

You need: ½ tsp moringa powder, 250ml water at ~75°C, 1cm ginger (sliced), 1 tsp honey.

  1. Steep ginger in warm water 2 minutes; remove or leave one slice.
  2. Stir in moringa using the paste-first method.
  3. Sweeten with honey off the boil.

Recipe 3: Moringa latte

You need: ½ tsp moringa powder, 150 to 180ml milk (oat, dairy, or almond), honey or maple, optional pinch of cinnamon.

  1. Mix powder with 1 to 2 tbsp cold milk until smooth.
  2. Heat remaining milk to about 65 to 70°C, hot to drink, not simmering.
  3. Pour onto paste, whisk. Sweeten; froth if you like.

Moringa leaf powder has no caffeine. For a caffeinated cup another morning, use Darjeeling black tea, a different product.

FAQ

How do you make moringa tea from powder?

Paste ½ tsp powder with a little water, top up with ~75°C water (or use the no-boil paste method), stir 20 to 30 seconds, finish with lemon, ginger, or milk.

Is moringa tea the same as moringa in food?

Same leaf powder, different serve. Tea is a drink; you can also stir the same powder into yoghurt, soup, or smoothies, see how to add moringa to your diet.

Does boiling water ruin moringa tea?

It does not ruin the powder, but it usually makes the cup more bitter. Use warm-not-boiling water or the cold paste method.

Does moringa tea have caffeine?

No. Moringa leaf powder is caffeine-free. Black tea and coffee are separate products.

How much powder per cup?

Start with ½ teaspoon per 250ml. Adjust to taste after you know the flavour.
