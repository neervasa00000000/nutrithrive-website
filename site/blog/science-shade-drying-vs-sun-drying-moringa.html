How to shade-dry moringa leaves at home

Wash the sprigs, then strip the leaves from the stems. Spread the leaves in a thin layer on mesh trays or a clean cloth, in the shade, with air moving across them. Leave them until they are crisp. Grind the crisp leaves, then store the powder in an airtight pouch away from steam.

Shade-dried or sun-dried: what you can check at home

Drying words on a label are a starting point, not proof. Open the pouch in daylight and use your eyes and nose. Then compare with the paperwork. Here's the side-by-side we use on our own batches.

What to checkWhat you'd hope to seeWhat makes us ask questions
ColourBright to medium green, even all through the pouchKhaki, olive-brown or patchy colour
SmellFresh, green, a bit like cut grass or spinachDusty, flat or musty
TextureFine and dry, with small soft clumps that break easilyHard lumps or any damp feel
LabelOne ingredient (moringa leaf), net weight, a pack or best-before date"Blend", no date, or no country of packing
PaperworkA lab summary you can open, ideally matched to a batch"Lab tested" with nothing to read

Colour and smell also change with harvest and age, so a dull pouch isn't automatically sun-dried. That's why the questions below matter.

Check your current powder in five steps

  1. Tip a teaspoon (about 3 g) onto a white plate in daylight and look at the colour.
  2. Smell it. Fresh leaf smells green. Old leaf smells of not much.
  3. Read the back of the pack: ingredients, net weight, pack or best-before date, and where it was packed.
  4. Look for the lab summary and check the date or batch it belongs to. Our lab testing guide shows what a useful one covers.
  5. Work out price per 100g: price ÷ grams × 100. Ours is $11 per 100g, or $10 per 100g in the 400g bundle.

FAQ

Is shade-dried moringa better than sun-dried?

Colour and smell are what you can check, not a winner. Bright green and a fresh grassy smell are good signs either way. Our family farm in Gujarat sun-dries the leaves, and we pack the powder in Truganina VIC.

How can I tell if moringa powder was shade-dried?

You can't tell for sure by looking. Bright green colour and a fresh smell are good signs, but harvest and age matter too. Ask the seller how they dry and mill, and check the pack date and lab summary.

Why is some moringa powder brown?

Usually age, heat or light exposure, or harsh drying. Brownish powder isn't necessarily unsafe, but it's a reason to check the date and ask questions.
