Searches for “moringa patches Australia”, Glorenda and Healrize usually want a transdermal patch. We sell leaf powder for meals and drinks, farm-grown, shade-dried, packed in Truganina. This page separates patch formats from food powder so you can choose deliberately.

Quick answer

  • Patches are a different product category from leaf powder food.
  • We do not sell patches and do not make medical claims about them.
  • Buy path: moringa powder for cooking and drinks.

What patches are

Moringa patches are adhesive products marketed for skin absorption. Brand names that appear in Australian search (including Glorenda and Healrize) change over time, check each seller’s own label and claims. We are not a reseller for those brands.

What we sell instead

Single-ingredient leaf powder. Use in smoothies, yoghurt, dal or tea. Taste notes: what moringa tastes like. Recipes: smoothie guide.
