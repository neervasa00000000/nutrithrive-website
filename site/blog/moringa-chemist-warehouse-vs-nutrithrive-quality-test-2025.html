What Chemist Warehouse usually stocks

Pharmacy ranges favour finished capsules and tablets because they fit a standard shelf bay and a dose on the label. A search for moringa chemist warehouse often surfaces capsule SKUs, not a 200g or 400g culinary pouch.

On 5 October 2026 the Forest Super Foods Pure Organic Moringa 120 capsules page was live on chemistwarehouse.com.au. We could not load a stable public price from that page (bot challenge), so confirm any dollar figure on the live listing before you buy. Capsule labels typically state milligrams per capsule and capsules per bottle. That is a different shopping problem from choosing a powder pouch by the gram.

If your local CW has a powder jar one week and empty pegs the next, that is normal for specialty lines. Check the app or store before you drive.

How to compare a CW bottle with a powder pouch

Use the label as a shopping list, not a medical scorecard:

  1. Ingredient list, look for moringa leaf (or Moringa oleifera leaf) and note fillers or blends.
  2. Amount per unit, mg per capsule × capsules in the bottle, or grams on a pouch.
  3. Pack size you can finish, a 100g pouch is a trial size; 400g is the large pouch on our product page.
  4. Price per 100g of leaf, convert the bottle or pouch to a common weight before you compare. We publish plain $/100g on the PDP (100g = $11.00/100g; 200g = $10.75/100g; 400g = $8.75/100g as of 6 Oct 2026).
  5. Origin and packing, farm or country of leaf, and where the pack was filled.

Skip "quality test" language unless you have a named, dated lab PDF you can link. NutriThrive publishes an NMI lab summary on the moringa powder product page (open the PDF).
