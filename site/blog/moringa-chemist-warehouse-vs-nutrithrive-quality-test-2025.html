Capsules and tablets: why we do not sell them

Capsules and tablets are convenient. They also hide dose maths behind "two capsules per day" language. A serve can be a few hundred milligrams of leaf, which is not the same as a teaspoon of powder (about 2 to 3 g).

NutriThrive chooses powder because you can see colour, smell the leaf, and measure with a spoon. If you specifically need a capsule shell for travel, buy that from a retailer that sells capsules. We will not invent a capsule product URL to chase moringa capsules chemist warehouse traffic.

Woolworths capsule searches

Some readers also search moringa capsules woolworths. Same rule: supermarket capsule stock is not our product. Compare the label (mg per serve, ingredient list, date) the same way you would at Chemist Warehouse. If you want powder instead of a supermarket capsule, use our pouch sizes below.

Label checks before you buy any CW pack

  • Format: powder, capsule, or tablet? Match the format to how you will actually use it.
  • Mg per serve: capsules per serve times mg per capsule.
  • Ingredient list: single leaf vs blend.
  • Date: best-before or pack date on the bottle or pouch.
  • Documents: a test claim you can open beats a badge with no file.

Those checks apply to any pack on a CW shelf. This article stays on the CW shopper decision versus our powder only.
