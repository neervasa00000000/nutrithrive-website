Moringa capsules at Chemist Warehouse: what’s in the bottle

Forest Super Foods Pure Moringa, 120 capsules

The listing gives 500 mg of moringa leaf powder per capsule and 120 capsules a bottle. That’s 60 g of leaf. It shows ARTG ID 460360, the Australian register number for listed medicines. Chemist Warehouse labels it as a Marketplace item, which means the brand sends it and delivery is charged separately at checkout.

Caruso’s Moringa 7000, 30 tablets

Each tablet contains 1 g (1,000 mg) of dry moringa leaf extract, made from 7 g of fresh leaf. That is where “7000” comes from. It’s a fresh-leaf equivalent, not 7 g of anything in the tablet. Because it’s a concentrated extract, comparing grams with plain leaf powder doesn’t tell you anything useful. Compare it with other extract products instead.

Does Chemist Warehouse sell moringa tablets?

Yes. Caruso’s Moringa 7000 (30 tablets) was listed at $14.39 on 9 November 2026, down from an RRP of $24.00. Use the store finder on the listing to see whether your local store has it.

How to work it out for any bottle

  1. Grams of leaf: capsule count × mg per capsule ÷ 1,000. For example, 120 × 500 ÷ 1,000 = 60 g.
  2. Price per 100g: shelf price ÷ grams × 100. For example, $79 ÷ 60 × 100 = $131.67.
  3. Skip “equivalent” numbers. If the label says “extract equivalent to” fresh or dry leaf, the sum doesn’t apply.

Capsules or a pouch: which one fits what you want?

They’re different products, so pick by format rather than by a headline number.

  • A pharmacy capsule comes with an ARTG number, a label with directions, and pickup or delivery alongside the rest of your Chemist Warehouse order.
  • A pouch of loose leaf powder is sold by weight. You get far more leaf for the money, but there’s no capsule and no pharmacy shelf.

If cost per gram of leaf is what you’re comparing, the pouch wins by a wide margin: $11 per 100g against about $132 for the capsule bottle above. If an ARTG-listed capsule is what you want, the pharmacy shelf is built for that, and this page won’t argue otherwise.

How to check any Chemist Warehouse moringa listing in five minutes

  1. Search “moringa” on the Chemist Warehouse site or app, then set your store or postcode.
  2. Look for a Marketplace line. It means a third-party seller ships the item and charges its own delivery.
  3. Open Ingredients and find the mg of moringa leaf per capsule, and whether it says “extract”.
  4. Work out grams of leaf and price per 100g using the steps above.
  5. Note the ARTG number, if there is one, and check it on the TGA’s public register.
