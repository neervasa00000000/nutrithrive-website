NutriThrive sells one ingredient: farm-grown moringa leaf powder, shade-dried, with an Australian NMI lab summary, packed in Truganina. Quick answer

  • CW greens: multi-ingredient scoop products; check the live CW label for organic marks and storage.
  • NutriThrive: single-ingredient leaf powder for food and drinks. Not certified organic.
  • Shop powder: moringa powder, use the sizes and prices shown on that page (100g, 200g, 400g).

What CW greens usually are

On 6 Oct 2026, Chemist Warehouse listed Vital Organic Greens Powder 200g Exclusive Size at $21.49. The published ingredient list is a certified-organic plant blend (spirulina, kale, broccoli, barley grass, wheat grass, chlorella) plus sweeteners under 1% each (stevia, monk fruit, thaumatin). Typical use on that page is teaspoon dosing in cold water or juice; storage often means refrigerate after opening and use within a stated window. Always re-check the live CW label before you buy, prices and copy change.

Other CW “greens” SKUs exist (for example Melrose and Raw Real Food listings). The same habit applies: open the product page, read the full ingredient list, and note fridge rules.

What moringa leaf powder is

Dried Moringa oleifera leaf only. Stir into smoothies, yoghurt, dal, eggs, or tea. See how to make moringa tea and moringa smoothie recipes. We shade-dry leaf from our farm, publish an NMI lab summary (PDF), and pack pouches in Truganina, Melbourne.

You want a branded multi-plant blend, you are fine following that product’s storage rules, and certified organic marks on the plant powders matter to you. Buy the CW SKU that matches your label checklist, we are not competing for that shopper by claiming organic.
