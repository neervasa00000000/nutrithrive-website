iHerb listings, what to check

iHerb search intent (“moringa iherb”) usually lands on imported powders or capsules. Before you checkout:

  • Ingredient: leaf only vs blend
  • Net grams and serving size for $/100g
  • Any organic or third-party seals, and what they actually certify
  • Shipping time and landed cost to Australia (product + freight)
  • Lot or CoA availability if metals matter to you

Amazon AU, what to check

“Amazon moringa powder” results mix marketplace sellers. Check:

  • Sold by / fulfilled by details
  • Ingredient panel photos, not just title keywords
  • Pack size in grams (titles can be misleading)
  • Recent reviews that mention colour, smell and packing date, still not a CoA
  • Return window if the bag arrives damaged
