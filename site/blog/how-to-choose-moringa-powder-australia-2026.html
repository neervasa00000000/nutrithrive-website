Moringa powder at Coles

On 9 November 2026, Coles’ online search showed no moringa leaf powder, capsules or tea. The results for “moringa” were unrelated “morning” products such as dishwashing liquid and muesli, which shows Coles had nothing that matched. A search for “wild earth moringa” also came back empty.

Store ranges can differ from the website, so if you’re near a big Coles, the app with your store selected is the final check. Going by the website, though, you can’t count on finding moringa at Coles.

Is Everyday Market the same as buying from Woolworths?

Not quite. An Everyday Market item is sold and shipped by a partner business. It arrives in its own parcel, the delivery fee and time are set by that seller, and the seller handles returns. You can’t pick it up in store with your groceries. Each listing names the seller, so look for that line before you add it to your trolley.

Does Woolworths sell moringa capsules or tea?

Not as plain items on 9 November 2026. The only capsule product in the search was a $158.96 bundle of two capsule bottles plus 120g of powder. “Moringa tea” mostly returned the same powders, oils and hair products. Coles had no moringa capsules or tea at all.

Moringa powder at Aldi

Aldi’s online search for “moringa” returned no results on 9 November 2026. Aldi’s Special Buys change every week, so something could turn up in a catalogue, but it isn’t a regular line.

Is the cheapest pouch the best buy?

Be honest about what the table shows: some marketplace powders cost less per 100g than ours, especially the 250g packs and the big bulk bags. Price per 100g is a good place to start, but it isn’t the whole story. Before you buy any pouch, check these five things:

  1. One ingredient. The label should say 100% moringa leaf (Moringa oleifera). A “greens” or “supergreens” mix is a different product.
  2. A date you can read. Look for a packed-on or best-before date. A 5kg bag only makes sense if you’ll finish it while it still smells fresh.
  3. Who ships it, and from where. Is it an Australian seller with an ABN and a street address, or a drop-shipper? Who do you contact if the parcel is damaged?
  4. Paperwork, and what it covers. Ask which lab tested the batch and what the report includes. A nutrition panel and a contaminant test are different reports, so ask which one you’re looking at.
  5. Delivered price. Add the seller’s delivery fee before you compare. A cheap 70g pouch with $10 postage isn’t cheap.
