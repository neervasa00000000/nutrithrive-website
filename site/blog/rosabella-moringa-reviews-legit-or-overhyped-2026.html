Is Rosabella moringa sold in Australia?

Not in shops. Rosabella’s product page says the moringa capsules are available “via our website, Amazon and TikTok shop”. Its “Where to buy” page lists its own store, Amazon and Walmart, and warns that anything else using the name isn’t theirs. We searched Chemist Warehouse’s website on 9 November 2026, and Rosabella wasn’t there.

So “Rosabella Australia” really means an overseas online order. Rosabella’s Australian store page prices in Australian dollars, but the parcel still comes from the US.

These prices don’t include shipping or import charges. Rosabella’s shipping policy (effective 6 November 2026) says it ships to most countries, delivery times vary, and customers pay any customs duties, taxes and import fees. Look at the delivered total in the cart before you pay.

The February 2026 recall: check your lot number

On 13 February 2026, Ambrosia Brands, the company behind Rosabella, announced a voluntary recall of certain lots of Rosabella Moringa Capsules, 60-count bottles, because of possible Salmonella contamination. The US FDA published the notice the same day.

What the notice says:

  • Which bottles: SKU starting with 1356, lot numbers ending in -1 or -2, and expiry dates between 03/2027 and 11/2027. The lot code is the middle seven digits of the code printed above the expiry date on the bottom of the bottle.
  • Where they went: sold in the US through tryrosabella.com and TikTok Shop. Rosabella’s recall page adds that products were sold in more than 80 countries, including the United Kingdom, Canada and Australia, and that unauthorised resale may have happened on sites such as eBay and Shein.
  • What to do: if your lot is listed, don’t use it, throw it away, and don’t sell or pass it on.
  • What they changed: the company says it stopped buying raw moringa leaf powder from the supplier linked to those lots.

The full list of affected lot numbers is on the FDA notice and on Rosabella’s recall page (both linked below). We couldn’t find a separate Australian recall notice when we searched on 9 November 2026. Rosabella’s own notice is what mentions Australia.

Is Rosabella moringa legit? What its reviews show

Rosabella is a real US company (Ambrosia Brands LLC) with a working store, a published shipping policy and a 90-day refund offer. Whether it’s “legit” usually means two things: will the order arrive, and do buyers stay happy?

What we could see on 9 November 2026:

  • Its own product page: 1,081 reviews. 65% were five-star, 7% four-star, 7% three-star, 4% two-star and 16% one-star.
  • What the one-star reviews say: several of the visible ones are about subscriptions, with people saying they had trouble cancelling repeat orders. If you order, check whether the checkout signs you up for repeat delivery.
  • Its Amazon claim: the product page says “Rated 4.3/5 on Amazon”. We didn’t check that figure on Amazon itself.

Reviews are personal accounts, not lab results. They can tell you about delivery and customer service, but not what’s in a batch.

Before you buy any overseas moringa brand

  • Delivered total: product + shipping + any import charges, divided by grams of leaf.
  • Lot and expiry: where they’re printed, and whether the brand has a recall page.
  • Repeat orders: whether the checkout adds a subscription by default.
  • Returns: who pays to send a parcel back to the US.
