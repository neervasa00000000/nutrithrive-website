Darjeeling tea at Woolworths

Woolworths lists Twinings Darjeeling Tea Bags, 10 pack. The ingredient is black tea, and it’s blended and packed in Poland for Twinings. When we checked on 9 November 2026 the page said “Available in store” but showed no online price without a store selected, so choose your store in the app to see it.

Specialist teas from a single named estate or a specific flush cost more, and if that’s what you’re after, they’re worth it. Our pouch is an everyday loose leaf Darjeeling from a family farm. It isn’t an estate-graded first flush, and we don’t price it like one.

Tea bags or loose leaf?

  • Bags are quick, and the right choice for a work drawer. The leaf is cut fine, so it brews fast and strong, and you can’t control it much.
  • Loose leaf lets you set the strength and steep good leaf a second time. It also shows the muscatel, slightly grape-like note Darjeeling is known for, especially when the water is below boiling.

At $7.50 for 100g, our loose leaf comes to about 15 cents a cup (100g makes about 50 cups).

What to check on the pack

  • “Darjeeling” on its own, not “Darjeeling-style” or a blend with other teas.
  • Flush, if it’s named: first flush (spring, light and floral), second flush (summer, fuller and muscatel) or autumnal (darker and rounder).
  • A packed-on or best-before date. Darjeeling is at its best while the aroma is fresh.
  • Loose or bagged, and the weight in grams, so you can compare price per 100g.

How to brew Darjeeling

  • One cup: 2g of leaf, water at 85–90°C, 3 to 4 minutes.
  • Teapot (1L): 8g, 85–90°C, 3 to 5 minutes.
  • With milk: 3g, 90–95°C, 4 to 5 minutes.

Boiling water makes Darjeeling harsh. Good leaf will give you a second, shorter steep. There’s more detail in our how to brew Darjeeling guide.
