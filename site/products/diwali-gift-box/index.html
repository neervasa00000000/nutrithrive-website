What's in the box

  • Darjeeling loose leaf black tea, 100g: About 50 cups. Floral, muscatel notes at 85 to 90°C. Contains caffeine, about 40 to 50 mg a cup.
  • Dried curry leaves, 30g: whole leaves for tadka. Use a little more than you would fresh, and taste as you go.
  • Handmade moringa & lavender soap, 95g: melt-and-pour base, moringa leaf, lavender fragrance, dried lavender flowers.

Who it's for: relatives you're visiting, a host who has plenty of mithai, neighbours, teachers and colleagues. For bulk orders, contact us.

Ingredients, origin and storage

Ingredients

Includes Darjeeling black tea, dried curry leaves (curry patta / kadi patta), and soap made with melt-and-pour soap base, moringa leaf, lavender fragrance and dried lavender flowers.

Origin

Family-farm tea from Darjeeling, farm-grown curry leaves, and soap handmade by us in Australia. Packed in Truganina, Melbourne.

How we make it

We pack all three in one box, with the soap wrapped separately so the tea doesn't take on its scent. Packed in Truganina. No moringa powder included.

Storage and shelf life

Keep the tea and curry leaves sealed and dry, and the soap on a draining dish.

Safety information

Soap is for external use only; patch-test if your skin is sensitive. Tea contains caffeine. Follow each product’s directions.
