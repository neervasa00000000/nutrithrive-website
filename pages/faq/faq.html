Moringa powder

What is NutriThrive Moringa powder?

It is 100% Moringa oleifera leaf powder from our own farm, with no fillers. We grow, harvest, shade-dry and manufacture it, arrange testing in Australia, and pack it in Truganina.

How much should I take daily?

Start with half a teaspoon a day for a week. Most adults then use 1 teaspoon (about 2 to 3 grams) once or twice a day if it sits well. Take it with food.

Any safety notes?

Leaf powder is widely eaten as food. Skip it if you are on blood thinners unless your GP agrees. If you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or on prescription medication, ask your healthcare provider first. Neer is not a doctor.

What does it taste like?

Earthy and a bit like spinach. In plain water most people dislike it. Mixed into food, most people barely notice it.

Is it lab tested?

Yes. Our moringa is tested in Australia. You can read the available summary PDF on the product page or contact us for testing details.

Dried curry leaves

What are NutriThrive dried curry leaves?

Karipatta grown on our own farm and packed in Melbourne. Use it in tadka for curries, dals and South Indian cooking.

How do I use dried curry leaves?

Use about 2 to 3 times the amount you would use fresh. Fry them in hot oil or ghee at the start of the pan. That is when the smell comes through.

How should I store them?

Airtight container, away from heat and light. They keep their flavour for 6 months or more in a cool, dry place.

Darjeeling tea

What is NutriThrive Darjeeling black tea?

Loose-leaf tea sourced from a family farm in Darjeeling, with a floral, muscatel character. We pack it in Melbourne and send it Australia-wide.

How do I brew it?

Use 1 to 2 teaspoons per cup. Water at 85 to 90°C, not a rolling boil. Steep 3 to 5 minutes. Plain, or with a splash of milk and honey.

How much caffeine is in a cup?

About 40 to 50 mg, similar to most black teas.
